# A Small-Budget Link Plan: What $100, $300 and $1,000 Actually Buy

Link building advice usually ignores the only question small sites have: what does my actual budget buy? So here are three real budgets, spent three sensible ways, using real store prices — PBN posts from $3, sidebar links from $4, guest posts from $150, mixed packages from $99.

**About $100 — the foundation month.** This buys a mixed starter package ($99) or roughly thirty PBN posts spread across different domains. Either way the goal is the same: a natural-looking base of brand and URL anchors pointing at the homepage and one or two key pages. Nothing fancy, everything reported. At this budget, one guest post would eat everything and leave the profile looking lopsided — foundations first.

**About $300 — base plus one real placement.** Keep the foundation (a smaller PBN/sidebar mix, roughly $100–150 worth) and add one guest post at the DR 30+ tier ($150) on a site in your niche, pointing at the page you most want to rank. This is the budget where a link profile starts to look like a profile instead of a purchase: volume underneath, one editorial link on top.

**About $1,000 — a campaign, not a purchase.** Now tiers open up: a large mixed package (the $899 tier) builds the wide base, or you assemble it yourself — a DR 50+ guest post ($290), a DR 30+ guest post ($150), and several hundred dollars of PBN posts and sidebar links spread over weeks, not days. At this level, pacing matters as much as selection: drip the links out, vary the anchors, and let each delivery report land before ordering the next batch.
